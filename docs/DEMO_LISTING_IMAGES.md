# Demo listing photography

The MC27-01–MC27-20 identities and rental descriptions are fictional development fixtures. Photos illustrate room types and are **not evidence of the listed Mauritius address, its layout, or its furnishings**. Images within a gallery may depict different homes; some secondary room photos are reused across listings. Replace these galleries with owner-authorized photography before using any record as genuine inventory.

All selected photos were sourced from Pexels, not rental marketplaces, agencies or private landlord listings. The [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) permits free use and modification in websites and apps. Attribution is recorded below; no creator endorsement is implied. These assets are included as part of this demo, not offered as a stock-photo collection.

Retrieved 2026-09-15. Original JPEGs are in `database/seeds/marketplace/images/`. Machine-readable provenance and gallery ordering: `database/seeds/marketplace/images.json`. There are 22 distinct photos, 20 distinct covers and 60 listing/image assignments (three per listing). Each listing has one cover and two supporting images. Costlier modern interiors are limited to the mid-market examples; lower-cost examples include compact rooms, basic kitchens and tiled interiors. Stock-photo availability limits whole-property and Mauritius-specific consistency.

The private storage path is `demo/mc27/<deterministic-property-uuid>/<filename>`. The public frontend receives only the existing backend's short-lived signed image URLs.
